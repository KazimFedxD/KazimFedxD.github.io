    AI Preneur '26 - Best Use of AI

    Best Use of AI · AI Preneur '26

    🏆

    • Full-stack production system: React 19 SPA + Django 5.2 + PostgreSQL + Redis + Celery
    • Judged Best Use of AI for the integration depth across STT, LLM, and TTS in a real product
