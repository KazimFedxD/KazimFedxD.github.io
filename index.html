Kazim Abbas — Backend / Applied AI Engineer · Karachi, Pakistan

Kazim Abbas, also indexed as Kasim Abbas and known online as FedxD, is a backend and applied-AI engineer based in Karachi, Pakistan. He builds production systems in Python, Django, and PostgreSQL, and ships voice-first interfaces powered by Deepgram, Groq, ElevenLabs, and Anthropic-compatible APIs.

His projects include TeachBack (winner of Best Use of AI at AI Preneur '26), Skyntel (2nd place at NASA Space Apps Challenge Karachi 2025), FedxD Data Container (FxDC), FxPy, FeXoBot, FxQuest, FinCore, Full-Stack Template, VoiceMatter, and Portfolio Website.
