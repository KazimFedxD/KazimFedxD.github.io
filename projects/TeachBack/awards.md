## About AI Preneur '26

AI Preneur is an initiative focused on fostering innovation at the intersection of artificial intelligence and entrepreneurship. Projects selected for AI Preneur '26 demonstrate:

- **Innovation**: Novel approaches to problem-solving with AI
- **Impact**: Potential to create meaningful change
- **Feasibility**: Technical soundness and development progress
- **Vision**: Clear understanding of target users and value proposition

TeachBack embodies these principles through its unique "learn by teaching" approach, leveraging AI not as a lecturer but as an engaged student that helps humans deepen their understanding.
